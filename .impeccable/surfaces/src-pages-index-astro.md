# Головна `/`

Scope: home page of the M Beauty One concept site. Visitor mode: Experience (register «вітрина»).

Audience and job: a woman in Lviv arriving from Instagram or a recommendation, mostly on a phone, checking whether the
salon is her level; then she finds her service and goes to bookon. Proof on hand: Marianna's studio shoot, phone photos
of the room and treats, Google rating 5,0 / 183 with named reviews, bookon prices. Constraints: first three blocks are
fixed (Marianna, salon and trust, services); no prices on the first screen; prices on the page in text size only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is one issue of a glossy magazine about this salon with its owner on the cover. It refuses the beauty
default: split hero, arches on cream, a row of equal cards and sections of one build.

OWN-WORLD: Espresso #3A251E fields and warm-grey #E9E8E4 paper spreads, a near-black colophon, brass only as the thin
line under the booking action. Upright condensed Noto Serif Display for masthead and headlines, Golos Text for reading.
Photo plates laid over oversized serif capitals; magazine credits instead of captions; no arches except the logo.

STORY: She sees Marianna, reads that this is her salon in central Lviv, sees the room and the hospitality with the
proof beside each claim, finds her service with its «від» price, and leaves for bookon.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Masthead «M BEAUTY ONE» spans the full width at the top. Marianna's 3:4 studio portrait, about 450 px
wide at 1440, sits just right of centre and overlaps the letters, her hair passing in front of them. H1 «Салон краси
в центрі Львова» bottom-left. Her name, role and the single booking link bottom-right. Address in the header. No
prices, counters or second button. Signature interaction: on scroll the portrait travels up over the masthead
(CSS scroll-driven). Motion grammar: plates settle, text never moves.

FORM: magazine issue; position 1 of the ordered list, chosen by the user as the pick over the assigned direction;
seed:c684db00033bf024.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Unresolved: none.
